Case study — 360º campaign

OAB/PE 2024 Campaign.

Visual identity and communication system for a presidential campaign at OAB Pernambuco, the state Bar Association, across digital, print and in-person.

Role
Art direction and design
Scope
Identity, digital, print and web
Year
2024
01 — Context

One identity for the whole campaign.

The campaign needed recognizable, consistent communication that could work across formats, channels and moments without losing visual unity.

I was responsible for creating the visual identity and developing the main graphic materials, connecting social media, WhatsApp, email marketing, the website, print, street actions and campaign headquarters communication.

The work was built as a system: one visual language adapted to informational pieces, mobilization, events, proposals and physical materials.

02 — Role

From concept to rollout.

01

Visual identity

Building the graphic language, art direction and defining the recurring elements of the campaign.

02

Digital communication

Pieces for social media, WhatsApp, event promotion, proposals, polls and mobilization.

03

Print materials

Flyers, stickers, badges, brochures and support materials for in-person campaigning.

04

Spaces and street

Applications for murals, headquarters, backdrops and the campaign’s physical touchpoints.

05

Web experience

Assets and visual guidelines for the website, keeping continuity between the online and offline campaign.

03 — Digital communication

04 — Campaign system

Content adapted to different formats.

Sequences, proposals, accountability reports and panoramic pieces, all in the same language.

05 — Applications

Materials designed to keep the campaign recognizable at every touchpoint.

06 — Support materials

Campaign stickers.

A series of short messages to expand presence, mobilization and visual recognition.
