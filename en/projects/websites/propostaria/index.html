WEBSITES / LANDING PAGE

Propostaria.

A landing page that shows the product working.

Landing page for Propostaria, a business-proposal app: visitors pick their trade, watch the sample proposal change, follow the timeline of an acceptance and try the signature right on the page.

Visit the websitepropostaria.com.br ↗

01 — THE PROJECT

Explain by showing.

Propostaria is an app for building, sending and tracking business proposals, where the client reads in the browser and accepts with a signature on the page itself. I built the landing page. The idea was to explain the product with the product: instead of screenshots, the page has a sample proposal that changes with the chosen trade, a timeline that follows a proposal from draft to acceptance, and a demo where the visitor signs and watches the status turn to "Accepted".

From design to code: HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with scroll animations (GSAP, ScrollTrigger and SplitText), smooth scrolling with Lenis, all switched off for people who prefer reduced motion, and deployed on Netlify.
