Email Generator.

From content to HTML email.

Free tools to build HTML emails that work in Gmail and Outlook and to generate WhatsApp links with a ready message.

Visit the websiteemailgenerator.com.br ↗

01 — THE PROJECT

Built to scale deliveries.

Email Generator was created to scale the team's campaign email production. The visual editor shows a desktop and mobile preview as you edit, saves your work in the browser and outputs HTML that works in Gmail and Outlook (VML), ready for the sending platform or to paste straight into Gmail.

Later it gained a second tool: a WhatsApp link generator with message formatting and a built-in shortener.
