Kompres.

Images and PDFs ready to use.

More than 30 image and PDF tools, from batch compression to PDF signing, all right in the browser without uploading files.

Visit the websitekompres.com.br ↗

01 — THE PROJECT

Born from a real need.

Kompres started in the routine of the CRM team I worked with, to end the manual PNG-to-WebP conversion before publishing every banner. With use, it became a box of more than 30 tools. For images: compress to an exact size in KB, resize, crop, rotate, add a watermark, blur areas, convert HEIC, SVG and favicons, make GIFs and, with AI, remove backgrounds and upscale. For PDFs: merge, split, organize, compress, sign, number pages and extract text.

Everything runs in the browser, AI included, without sending files to a server. The tools start working as soon as you drop the files, accept pasting with Ctrl+V and download everything at once, as separate files, into a folder or as a ZIP.
