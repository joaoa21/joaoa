WEBSITES / DIGITAL PRODUCT

Clube de Ouro.

A loyalty program that explains itself through play.

The interface of an iGaming platform’s loyalty program: ten levels with characters, a leaderboard with prizes, a rewards store and a benefits comparison.

Explore the experienceInteractive experience ↗

01 — THE PROJECT

From concept to the player’s screen.

Clube de Ouro is the gamified loyalty program of an iGaming platform, and it went live for players. I designed and coded the whole experience: the journey through ten levels, from Paper King to Emperor, the two point counters (XP to level up and Leaderboard to compete), the store where Nuggets turn into rewards and the comparison of what each level unlocks.

The copy came from the content team, and the levels and rewards from the casino lead. Everything else, from layout to code, was mine: HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with a level carousel, FAQs filtered by topic and a mobile version.

02 — THE EXPERIENCE

The player’s journey in three steps.

/01

Play

Every eligible activity earns points automatically, with no sign-up.

/02

Level up

XP Points take players through the ten levels, each with its own mini-game and new perks.

/03

Compete and redeem

Leaderboard Points compete for daily, weekly and monthly prizes, and Nuggets become rewards in the store.
