WEBSITES / CORPORATE

MS Corretora.

Trust on the first screen, a quote on the next.

Corporate website for an insurance brokerage: it presents the coverage options and partner insurers and takes visitors from interest to a WhatsApp quote.

Visit the websitemscorretoraseguros.com.br ↗

01 — THE PROJECT

Credibility that turns into contact.

MS Corretora has worked for over 15 years with life, auto, home, motorcycle, travel and business insurance. The website had two jobs: convey the credibility of an established brokerage and turn interest into contact. The brand colors, navy and orange, guided the interface: a dark top, light cards for the coverage options and orange reserved for the calls to action.

From design to code: HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with scroll animations (GSAP and Lenis), a products page, an FAQ, social sharing metadata and custom error pages, deployed on Netlify.

02 — THE EXPERIENCE

From interest to a quote in three steps.
