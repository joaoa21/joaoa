Venda
Bar Arretado.

Come on in, boss.

Venda is a corner bar that feels like a backcountry general store from Brazil's sertão. The brand cuts out its name by hand, like a painted sign, and scatters suns, cacti, moons and hummingbirds across the menus and coasters.
